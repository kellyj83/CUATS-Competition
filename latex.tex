\section{Methodology Overview}
The \texttt{CombinedStrategy} is an ensemble quantitative model designed to provide diversified exposure through three distinct sub-strategies: Defensive Adaptive Asset Allocation (DAA), Kalman-based Pairs Trading, and a static Gold anchor. The model utilizes a hierarchical leverage structure to enhance returns while maintaining defensive guardrails.

\section{Core Components}

\subsection{Defensive Adaptive Asset Allocation (DAA)}
The DAA component serves as the primary growth engine. It utilizes a "Canary" mechanism to detect regime shifts.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Canary Assets:} VWO (Emerging Markets) and BND (Total Bond Market).
    \author \textbf{Momentum Scoring:} Assets are ranked using a weighted momentum formula:
    \begin{equation}
    12\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-22}} - 1\right) + 4\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-63}} - 1\right) + 2\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-126}} - 1\right) + \left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-252}} - 1\right)
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Defensive Logic:} If all canary assets exhibit negative momentum, 100\% of the DAA slice moves to IEF (Intermediate Treasuries).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Statistical Arbitrage (Pairs Trading)}
The strategy identifies price dislocations between correlated assets using a state-space model.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Kalman Filter:} Dynamically estimates the hedge ratio $\beta$ and intercept $\alpha$ for the linear relationship $y_t = \beta x_t + \alpha + \epsilon_t$.
    \item \textbf{Z-Score Entry:} Trades are signaled when the residual $\epsilon_t$ deviates significantly from its moving standard deviation:
    \begin{equation}
    Z = \frac{\text{spread} - \mu_{\text{spread}}}{\sigma_{\text{spread}}}
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\section{Portfolio Construction}
The portfolio is rebalanced monthly for DAA and daily for Gold. Leverage is applied at the sub-strategy level.
